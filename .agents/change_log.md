Settings > Mail > NEW MAIL ALERTS (OFF / EVERY 15 MIN) asks Android for notification permission, then schedules a WorkManager job (`workmanager`) that runs `mailAlertDispatcher` in a background isolate: `MailAlertRunner.check()` reads the inbox fresh (the Gmail `in:inbox` list on Gmail), compares with a cursor kept in LocalStore (`planMailAlerts`: unread and newer; the first look and a change of account only set the cursor, so turning it on does not announce the inbox) and shows a notification per message through `flutter_local_notifications`. - 2026-10-03: New-mail notifications, step 2 of 2 (INSTANT), awaiting the user's phone test. `MailAlertMode.instant` starts a foreground service (`flutter_foreground_task`, `foregroundServiceType="specialUse"` because `dataSync` is cut to 6 h a day on Android 15) whose task handler runs a `MailIdleLoop`: look at the inbox, then `MailService.waitForChange` (IMAP IDLE on All Mail on Gmail, else the inbox; 25 min at most, since servers drop an idle connection after about half an hour), look again on a change or a timeout; a failing connection is retried after 30 s, 1, 2, then 5 min. The 15-minute WorkManager look is kept running in this mode as a net for when Android stops the service. Choosing INSTANT also asks Android to leave the launcher out of battery saving (`REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`) and says if that was refused. The service shows Android's permanent notification (low importance).
